Marvel–designing a design tool

Marvel is a prototyping, collaboration and design platform. I worked on the product team as a design intern, improving the new design tool.

My main focus was asset creation; designing and implementing pixel perfect UI assets and wireframes to be reused by other designers.

A few weeks into my internship I went to Antwerp, Belgium, to speak at a conference about how we work at Marvel.

Feel free to reach out if you have any questions about my work at Marvel.
