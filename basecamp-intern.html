Actionable Blank Slates

When starting a new project in Basecamp, you are faced with a bunch of tools to pick from. Each tool has a lot of copy explaining what it's for, but there are no clear actions. (You have to click on the card to get started, but we don't state that anywhere.)

I wanted to take a stab at making it feel easier to jump in–simply by making these blank slates actionable. I designed and built an A/B test.
